## Central Tendencie

### Mean | Median | Mode

- **Mean** is sensitive tword outliers (asymmetrical datasets)
- **Median** is less sensitive to outliers
- **Mode** is good for categorical values

## Dispersion

### Skewness (Asymmetry)

- **Positive skewness (Right Skewed)**: Most of the values on a frequency chart are on the left side and the outliers on the right side. Usually is Mode < Median < Mean
- **Negative skewness (Left Skewed)**: Most of the values on a frequency chart are on the right side and the outliers on the left side. Usually is Mean < Median < Mode

On the calculus of Skewness if it is positive, then is positive skew, if negative is negative skew.
